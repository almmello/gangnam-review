## Identity and source content

The rose palette and editorial typography were studied on Gangnam Beauty
Guide. The GR symbol, Gangnam Review wordmarks, layout, favicon, and social
card are original compositions for this independent prototype.
No original Gangnam Beauty Guide logo, clinic images, or patient-review dataset
is bundled in the repository. The application retrieves published text on demand and
can export it locally at the user's request. Source links and attribution remain
